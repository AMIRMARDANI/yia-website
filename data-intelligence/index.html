Use English column names and units. Collect only variables justified by the research question. Avoid names, addresses, medical record numbers, and other direct identifiers. Skin colour and location can be sensitive and potentially bias-prone; include them only with a clear scientific justification and appropriate consent/governance.

1. Add data

Upload a CSV or Excel file, or enter one numeric observation. The file is inspected in your browser before saving.

CSV, TSV, JSON, Excel and SAS XPT are supported. XPT files are read locally with the same Python/pandas engine used in Research Workspace. CSV sample · JSON sample · TSV sample · Excel sample

Images are not analysed on this page yet. Record the image type and submit it for human review; image-analysis support will be added as a separate, validated module.
